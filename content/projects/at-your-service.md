*at your service* was made as a semester installation project at IIT Bombay in 2019. It uses a conversational interface to explore the dynamics of service — who serves, who is served, and what gets lost in the transaction.

The piece uses an Eliza-style chatbot as its core, but the conversation is shaped to surface the absurdity and intimacy embedded in the idea of being "at service."
